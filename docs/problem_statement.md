# Problem Statement

Students in the JAMB Success Campus Programme by Community Digital Innovation Hub lack dedicated applications that they can use to practice effectively for their JAMB examination. Although, there are various academic apps like TestDriller, ExamGuide, MySchool, etc. that they can use to practice for JAMB, this apps applications are usually paid for. In other to make FREE applications available for students who can not afford the paid apps, CDIH is creating a FREE application that students can use to prepare for JAMB.

## Features in the application

### First version

- Study subjects include Math, English, Chemistry, Physics and Biology which are currently taught in the JAMB Success Campus
- JAMB study questions from past years with structured and clear answers
- The application must run offline
- Can be downloaded from Google Play Store or the browser if funds are not sufficient enough to be uploaded on Google Play Store
- Clean UI/UX to ensure secondary school students are able to use it effectively

Note:

- Students are only able to study with this version, that is after every question you get the correct answer if you want. There is no mock or practice section available yet.
